## Caveats to know before presenting
- **IIT Kanpur shares** are taken from a press summary of the 2016 report. Other summaries give vehicles 20–25%.
- **Official vs independent sanitation figures** do not measure the same thing. The 12 crore toilets and ODF Plus counts are administrative (self-reported) data. NSS 2018 measured *access*, and it was carried out before the 2019 ODF declaration.
- **Recent Delhi numbers come from news reports** and vary by date: the bus fleet split, the CNG price and Ghazipur's remaining waste.
